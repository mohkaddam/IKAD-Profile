Company Overview

Engineering excellence rooted in Sudan

IKAD Engineering was established in February 2018 in Khartoum, Sudan, by a group of highly skilled engineers. Recognized across the building construction and infrastructure sectors, IKAD is a privately held company headquartered in Khartoum Al-Manshiya, Block 25, Building No. 3, Third Floor.

Through diverse experience in the construction market, the company has built its knowledge, skills and human resources to meet international standards of time, quality and safety management.

Core Specialty

Building construction, finishing & fit-out

Our core specialty is building construction, finishing and fit-out works — from structural shell construction through complete interior finishing and fit-out. We complement this with in-house fire protection, HVAC, plumbing and equipment supply capabilities, delivered by two working directors bringing more than 10 years of combined industry experience.

Our Vision

“To be a respectable building contractor delivering beyond expectation, always — providing the premier level of service to our community, supported by solid expertise through innovation, proficiency, and association.”

Our Mission

“IKAD is devoted to procuring projects at competitive pricing, providing safe working conditions, and delivering quality work within reasonable time frames — giving the country the highest level of building construction, fire protection, life safety, and security.”
