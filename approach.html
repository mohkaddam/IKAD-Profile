Our Approach

The vision, mission and capabilities that guide every project we deliver.

Our Vision

“To be a respectable building contractor delivering beyond expectation, always — providing the premier level of service to our community, supported by solid expertise through innovation, proficiency, and association.”

Our Mission

“IKAD is devoted to procuring projects at competitive pricing, providing safe working conditions, and delivering quality work within reasonable time frames — giving the country the highest level of building construction, fire protection, life safety, and security.”

Capabilities

What we bring to every project

  • Core specialty: building construction, finishing and civil works, from structural shell to completion
  • Core specialty: complete interior fit-out, including finishes, fixtures and services integration
  • Design, installation, inspection and maintenance of fire hydrant, sprinkler, water mist, foam and FM-200 clean agent systems
  • Supply and installation of firefighting pump sets, including FM-approved / UL-listed and containerized configurations
  • Supply and installation of HVAC and air conditioning equipment from internationally recognized brands
  • Water supply pipework, water storage tank installation and pump systems
  • Equipment sourcing and supply through established international supplier relationships
