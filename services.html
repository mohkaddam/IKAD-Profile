Our Services

Our core specialty is building construction, finishing and fit-out works. We complement this with in-house fire protection, HVAC and plumbing capabilities, allowing us to deliver complete turnkey projects.

Core Specialty

Building Construction, Finishing & Fit-Out

Building construction, finishing and fit-out works are IKAD's core specialty — from structural shell construction through complete interior finishing and fit-out for commercial and institutional facilities.

Structural & Shell Construction

Construction of building shells for new branch and facility projects.

Finishing Works

Complete interior and exterior finishing works, including wall, floor and ceiling finishes, as delivered on our completed branch projects.

Interior Fit-Out

Complete interior fit-out including finishes, teller counters, waiting areas and services integration, as delivered on our bank branch projects.

Complementary Service

Fire Protection & Life Safety Systems

Design, installation, inspection and maintenance of complete fire protection systems for commercial, residential, industrial and institutional facilities. IKAD has over 10 years of experience in this discipline, delivered in-house alongside our core construction and fit-out projects.

Fire Hydrant Systems

A large variety of hydrant valves supplied and installed to client specifications, with inspection across multiple parameters and timely, cost-effective delivery.

Automatic Fire Sprinkler Systems

Design, installation, inspection, servicing and maintenance of automatic sprinkler systems that react quickly to reduce heat, flame and smoke.

Water Mist Fire Suppression Systems

Fine water-spray systems that control, suppress or extinguish fire by cooling flame and gases, displacing oxygen, and attenuating radiant heat.

Foam Extinguishing Systems

Fixed foam proportioning and discharge equipment for fuel and chemical storage tanks and bunds, refineries and petrochemical installations, chemical process plants, loading racks, and airport or helicopter landing areas.

Foam Equipment — Bladder Tanks

Bladder tank proportioning systems that inject foam concentrate into the water supply automatically, across a wide range of flows and pressures, without external power.

FM-200 Clean Agent Suppression

Clean agent suppression for people, sensitive facilities and irreplaceable assets — applied in computer suites, telecom facilities, railway signaling centers, archives and laboratories, air traffic management centers, petrochemical plants, offshore oil and gas installations, pipeline pumping stations, ship machinery spaces, and rail rolling stock.

Fire Fighting Pump Sets

Reliable pump packages ensuring adequate water supply at required pressure in emergency conditions: FM-approved / UL-listed sets, standard sets, multi-stage outlet (MSMO) sets, and containerized sets.

Complementary Service

HVAC & Air Conditioning

Supply and installation of air conditioning and mechanical ventilation systems for commercial and institutional buildings, working with internationally recognized equipment brands including Daikin, LG, O General, Midea and Prime Air Conditioning Industries.

Ducted & Cassette AC Systems

Supply and installation of ceiling cassette units, ducting and diffusers for commercial interior spaces, as delivered on our completed branch fit-out projects.

Branded Equipment Supply

Sourcing of air conditioning equipment from recognized global manufacturers to match project specifications and budgets.

Complementary Service

Plumbing & Water Supply Systems

Pipework, water supply infrastructure and pump installation for commercial and industrial facilities, including underground pipe laying and water storage tank installation.

Water Supply Pipework

Installation of underground and above-ground water supply lines for new buildings and facilities.

Water Storage Tank Installation

Supply and installation of water storage tanks to support building water supply and fire protection reserves.

Pump Supply & Installation

Water pump equipment sourced through supplier relationships including Pedrollo and GESPASA Fluid Technology (Spain).

Complementary Service

Supply of Engineering Equipment

As a channel for recognized international manufacturers, IKAD sources and supplies fire protection, HVAC and plumbing equipment for contractors, consultants and end clients across Sudan.
