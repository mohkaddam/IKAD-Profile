Who We Are

A privately held engineering contractor, built by skilled engineers

IKAD Engineering was established in February 2018 in Khartoum, Sudan, by a group of highly skilled engineers. Recognized across the building construction and infrastructure sectors, IKAD is headquartered in Khartoum Al-Manshiya.

Through diverse experience in the construction market, we have built our knowledge, skills and human resources to meet international standards of time, quality and safety management.
